Heritage & Pedigree

Craftsmanship Refined Over 4 Decades

The foundation of Ram Keval Interior was laid by our master craftsman, whose journey began in 1985. With years of dedicated work in the furniture industry across prominent areas of Mumbai, including Navi Mumbai and West Mumbai, he honed his skills and led expert artisan teams with unmatched precision.

1985 • Mumbai Roots

Artisanal foundation across Navi & West Mumbai; mastery in solid woodwork, fine joinery, and large residential commissions.

2000 • Landmark Works in Kathmandu

Brought expertise to Nepal, contributing to prestigious landmarks like KL Residency in Sano Gaucharan, and projects in Thamel and Naxal.

Corporate Trust • Triveni & Vishwakarma

Associated with major industrial conglomerates including Triveni Group and Vishwakarma Cement for premium infrastructure.

2025 • Professional Painting Expansion

Continuing the legacy of innovation, broadened services to include turnkey painting to deliver complete luxury spaces under one roof.
